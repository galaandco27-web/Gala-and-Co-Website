ABOUT GALA & CO

Gala & Co represents a structured approach to real estate and construction, where every decision is backed by insight and every project is executed with precision.

Based in Pune and Mumbai, the firm works with developers, investors, end users and with the NRI segment, delivering structured support across residential and commercial property transactions. This includes supporting clients in buying, selling, and leasing residential and commercial properties, along with new and resale transactions to investment-led acquisitions, the focus remains on clarity, due diligence and value-driven decision making.

Gala & Co also partners with developers on a mandate basis including sole selling assignments, taking ownership of project sales and helping streamline the overall sales process. From project positioning and buyer outreach to deal closure, the approach is designed to create efficiency, consistency and stronger outcomes.

Gala & Co also undertakes contracting with a specialization in RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) works for residential and commercial developments, along with infrastructure development. Each assignment is handled with a strong emphasis on execution quality, site discipline and adherence to timelines.

By combining real estate expertise with on-ground execution, Gala & Co offers a more cohesive approach ensuring that decisions are not only well-informed but also effectively implemented.

ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Maitri K. Gala is the Founder of Gala & Co, leading the firm across real estate and construction.

With experience in the industry, she has previously worked with Nyati Group, Pune, where she was involved in luxury and international sales, as well as business development. This exposure has contributed to her understanding of high-value transactions, client handling, and market positioning.

Her work is focused on building a more structured approach to real estate one that combines deal-making with execution, instead of treating them as separate functions. She is directly involved in developer mandates, high-value transactions and contracting projects, ensuring that every aspect is handled with control and clarity.

Over time she has built strong working relationships with developers, investors and the NRI market, positioning Gala & Co as a firm that is not just involved in transactions, but in how projects are positioned, sold, and delivered.

Her focus is clear: build a firm that operates with intent, maintains high standards, and delivers consistently across both real estate and construction.

OUR SERVICES

At Gala & Co, we offer a comprehensive range of services across real estate and construction, designed to support clients at every stage from investment decisions to on-ground execution.

REAL ESTATE

Primary Sales

We facilitate transactions in new residential and commercial developments working closely with both developers and clients. Our focus is on helping clients secure the right property at the right price, ensuring transparency, market alignment and a smooth end-to-end buying experience.

Project Mandates

Gala & Co partners with developers on exclusive project mandates, taking ownership of the sales process. Our role extends beyond transactions we support project positioning, marketing, buyer outreach and deal closure, helping developers streamline sales and improve overall efficiency.

Commercial Leasing

We support clients in securing the right commercial spaces based on their operational and investment requirements. We also work with brands, corporates and MNCs to support their expansion by identifying and securing the right commercial spaces. Our approach focuses on location strategy, property selection and transaction support to ensure long-term value for business operations.

Investment Advisory

We provide strategic real estate investment guidance to clients, including NRIs across the globe. With a strong understanding of the Pune and Mumbai markets, we help investors identify the right opportunities, ensuring informed decisions aligned with long-term growth and returns.

CONTRACTING

RCC Works

We specialize in RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) contracting for residential and commercial developments. Our scope also includes lock-and-key (turnkey) projects, ensuring seamless execution from structural work to project completion, with a strong focus on quality and timelines.

Interior Fit-Outs

We undertake interior fit-out projects, delivering functional and well-executed spaces tailored to client requirements. Our work ensures smooth coordination across design, materials, and on-site execution.

Infrastructure Development

Gala & Co is involved in infrastructure-related projects, focusing on efficient planning, coordination, and execution while maintaining required quality and compliance standards.

Project Management

We provide end-to-end project management services, overseeing planning, coordination, and execution. Our role is to ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget, and as per defined quality standards.
